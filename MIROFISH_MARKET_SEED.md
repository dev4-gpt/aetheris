# Aetheris market scenario seed for MiroFish

**Purpose:** adversarial discovery of buyer objections and interview questions. This is input to a synthetic simulation, not evidence of market demand. Prepared 23 October 2026; no official MiroFish run has been executed.

## Verified product state

Aetheris is an unclassified local pilot for importing public or operator-provided observations, showing source and freshness, viewing a bounded U.S. terrain scene, and saving/replaying/exporting human decision records. Weather, earthquake, event, satellite prediction, and limited regional AIS adapters exist. Flights, CCTV, and commercial cable data remain permission-gated. The remote production pilot, customer integration, and restore/load validation are incomplete. There is no verified customer, paid pilot, revenue, operational defense deployment, or measured time-saving result. See [README](README.md), [release gates](RELEASE_GATE_CHECKLIST.md), and [startup scorecard](STARTUP_BUILDABILITY_SCORECARD.md).

## Market facts and alternatives

- [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-11/Reliability%2C%20Resilience%20and%20Risk.pdf) reports utility resilience-plan requirements in 14 states and one city as of June 2024.
- [FEMA RAPT](https://preptoolkit.fema.gov/web/hazard-explorer) provides free GIS resilience planning tools.
- [Esri](https://www.esri.com/en-us/industries/emergency-management/solutions/situational-awareness) provides emergency-management dashboards, real-time feeds, impact assessment, and field coordination.
- [Everbridge](https://www.everbridge.com/products/visual-command-center/) provides an enterprise visual command center for risk events and organizational assets.
- No public price comparison, Aetheris customer survey, or addressable-market estimate has been validated.

## Hypothetical offer to challenge

Buyer: a business-continuity or emergency-operations director at a multi-site U.S. infrastructure operator. Job: after a severe-weather event, connect permitted observations to facilities, review source and age, and export a replayable decision record. A proposed paid pilot is $5,000 for one workflow over four to six weeks. Annual offers to test are $12,000 for one small team and $24,000 for a multi-site team. These are proposed prices, not established willingness to pay.

## Simulation request

Run five skeptical roles: buyer, duty operator, GIS incumbent advocate, IT/security reviewer, and finance/procurement reviewer. Explore the following conditions separately:

1. The team already uses email, a spreadsheet, FEMA RAPT, and Esri.
2. The workflow consumes 5, 10, or 25 team-hours per week.
3. Aetheris saves 0%, 15%, or 30% of the time with no increase in missed evidence.
4. A desired live feed cannot legally be used, so it appears unavailable.
5. The customer can import a redacted facility list but cannot provide sensitive operations data.

For each condition, output: objections, what evidence would change a decision, who controls budget, likely integration/security work, a question to ask a real buyer, and the simplest falsifiable pilot. Do **not** output adoption scores, cohort win rates, forecast revenue, defense endorsements, purchase intent, or a claim that the simulated roles are real people.

## Interpretation

Use the run to plan interviews. Update the [market model](AETHERIS_MARKET_RESEARCH_2026.md) only with real interviews, timed workflow tests, signed scopes, actual costs, or contracts.
